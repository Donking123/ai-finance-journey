DBS Group Holdings Q1 2025 Earnings Press Release

DBS Group Holdings Ltd reported its financial results for the first quarter ended 31 March 2025.

Total income rose 5% year-on-year to S$5.42 billion, from S$5.17 billion in Q1 2024.
Net profit declined 2% to S$2.89 billion, compared to S$2.96 billion a year ago.
Net interest margin was 2.12%, down 2 basis points from 2.14% in Q1 2024.
Fee income surged 17% to S$1.08 billion, driven by wealth management and card fees.
Cost-to-income ratio improved to 38.9% from 39.4%, reflecting disciplined cost management.
Non-performing loan ratio remained stable at 1.1%.
Common Equity Tier 1 ratio stood at 14.8%, well above regulatory requirements.
The Board declared a dividend of S$0.60 per share, up from S$0.54 in Q1 2024.

CEO Piyush Gupta commented: "Our diversified franchise delivered resilient results despite a challenging rate environment. Strong fee income growth offset modest NIM compression, and our capital position supports continued shareholder returns."
