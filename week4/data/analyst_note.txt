Analyst Note: CapitaLand Integrated Commercial Trust (CICT) — Maintain BUY

We maintain our BUY rating on CICT with a target price of S$2.35, representing 12% upside from the current price of S$2.10. CICT remains our top Singapore REIT pick for 2025.

Portfolio occupancy improved to 97.2% in Q1 2025, up from 96.5% in Q4 2024, driven by strong leasing momentum at Raffles City Singapore and CapitaSpring. Positive rental reversions of +8.3% signal robust tenant demand. Aggregate leverage stands at 39.1%, providing comfortable headroom below the 50% regulatory limit.

Key risks include slower-than-expected rate cuts delaying cap rate compression, potential tenant defaults in the retail segment, and foreign exchange exposure from the Australian and German properties. However, CICT's diversified portfolio across office, retail, and integrated developments provides resilience. The current distribution yield of 5.4% remains attractive relative to the 10-year Singapore Government Securities yield of 3.1%.
